What I am known for

Four things people hire me for.

Not a job description. These are the patterns that show up in every team I have run and every product I have shipped.

Turning execution teams into strategic ones

Design here was a UI service desk. Rebuilding it took a practice with its own research, its own standards, and a seat in the product decisions that matter. That rebuild is the work I am proudest of, and the team owns it now.

Making AI earn its place

The team embeds AI across research, ideation and delivery, then cuts whatever does not hold up. Setting that bar is my job. It speeds the work and keeps data heavy systems trustworthy. Judgment stays with people.

Designers who ship, not hand off

I built a design engineering practice: designers sit with customers, then fix the UI in the codebase and deploy it themselves. No handoff, no ticket queue, no waiting a sprint for a four pixel problem. I still design too, and a working prototype inside 48 hours when a deal needs one.

Systems over heroics

Shared standards and a single research process took design cycles from two weeks to four days, and research from three weeks to a week and a half. Repeatable beats heroic.
